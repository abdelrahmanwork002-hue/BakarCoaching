# Calisthenics Progression Matrices & Skill Trees

A calisthenics system relies entirely on altering **leverage, mechanical disadvantage, and weight distribution** rather than adding external iron weight. Use these matrices to scale movements precisely to client capabilities.

---

## 1. The Pull-Up Tree (Vertical Pulling)
Move down the matrix as structural capacity increases. Never progress a client if form degrades.

```text
Level 1: Scapular Activations (Hangs with active retraction and depression)
   └── Level 2: Inverted Horizontal Rows (Bar at chest height, feet on ground)
         └── Level 3: Eccentric/Negative Pull-ups (5-second controlled descents)
               └── Level 4: Strict Pull-ups (Full dead-hang to chest-to-bar)
                     └── Level 5: Weighted Pull-ups OR L-Sit Pull-ups
                           └── Level 6: The Clean Explosive Muscle-Up
```

| Progression Stage | Primary Cues | Key Milestone for Next Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Active Rows** | Pull bar to lower ribs, pack shoulder blades | 3 sets of 12 strict, slow reps |
| **Stage 2: Negatives** | Jump up, fight gravity all the way down | 4 sets of 5 reps (6s eccentric each) |
| **Stage 3: Volume Pulls** | Drive elbows into pockets, hollow body shape | 3 sets of 8 strict dead-hang reps |
| **Stage 4: L-Sit Pulls** | Keep legs parallel to floor, toes pointed | 3 sets of 6 deep chest-to-bar reps |

## 2. The Push-Up to Planche Tree (Anterior Push)

<Image src="image_agent_tag_10800639120369010929" alt="Athlete performing a clean horizontal push-up progression focusing on structural alignment" caption="Push-up and planche alignment basics" />

*   **Phase 1: Incline Push-ups** → Build standard tendon density.
*   **Phase 2: Strict Floor Push-ups** → Hollow body, protracted scapulae at the top.
*   **Phase 3: Diamond Push-ups** → Increased triceps and central chest load.
*   **Phase 4: Pseudo Planche Push-ups (PPPU)** → Hands backward, leaning forward to shift the center of gravity.
*   **Phase 5: Tuck Planche Hold** → Knees to chest, hips level with shoulders, maximum scapular protraction.

## 3. The Handstand Push-up Tree (Vertical Pushing)
1. **Wall Headstand Hold:** Building basic inverted vascular tolerance (Target: 45 seconds).
2. **Pike Push-ups (Feet on Floor):** Learning the tripod path (Head lands forward of hands).
3. **Elevated Pike Push-ups:** Feet on a 50cm box, torso stacked perfectly vertical over hands.
4. **Wall-Assisted Handstand Push-ups (Chest-to-Wall):** Eliminates banana back, enforces core lock.
5. **Freestanding Handstand Push-up:** Peak neurological and balance requirement.

---

## 4. Mastery Benchmarks for Advanced Skills
Do not program specialized skill work (Planche, Front Lever, Human Flag) until these prerequisites are met effortlessly:
* [ ] **15 Strict Chest-to-Bar Pull-ups**
* [ ] **25 Clean Parallel Bar Dips**
* [ ] **20 Deep, Slow Pike Push-ups**
* [ ] **60-Second Dead Hang + 60-Second Hollow Body Rock**
