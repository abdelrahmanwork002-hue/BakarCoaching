# Asana Progression Trees & Structural Modification Matrices

Progress in a holistic yoga practice is measured by **sustainability of breath, depth of mindfulness, and spatial alignment** rather than forcing a joint past its natural anatomical stops.

---

## 1. Spinal Extension & Backbending Tree
Scale these movements to ensure the lumbar spine does not pinch or collapse under compression.

```text
Level 1: Salabhasana (Locust Pose - Active posterior muscular chain extension)
   └── Level 2: Bhujangasana (Baby Cobra - Scapular retraction, zero hand pressure)
         └── Level 3: Urdhva Mukha Svanasana (Upward Dog - Femurs lifted, active glutes)
               └── Level 4: Ustrasana (Camel Pose - Supported hips pushing forward)
                     └── Level 5: Dhanurasana (Bow Pose - Dynamic kick against hand resistance)
                           └── Level 6: Urdhva Dhanurasana (Full Wheel Pose - Deep thoracic opens)
```

| Progression Stage | Active Alignment Cues | Milestone for Advanced Variation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Baby Cobra** | Press tops of feet down, pull chest forward | 5 slow, deep breaths with zero hand weight |
| **Stage 2: Sphinx Hold** | Pull elbows back isometrically, lift belly button | 2 minutes sustained without shoulder dumping |
| **Stage 3: Supported Camel**| Hands support sacrum, lengthen lumbar up first | Open thoracic chest without neck collapsing |
| **Stage 4: Full Wheel** | Parallel feet, drive through heels, wrap triceps | Arms completely locked out, head hanging loose |

## 2. Inversion Progressions (Overhead System)

<Image src="image_agent_tag_14167393435422849156" alt="Yogi holding a stable forearm stand alignment demonstrating core integration and shoulder stability" caption="Inversion and core integration principles" />

*   **Phase 1: Viparita Karani (Legs Up Wall)** → Introduces basic circulatory drainage and autonomic reset.
*   **Phase 2: Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward Dog)** → Core compression and shoulder weight bearing.
*   **Phase 3: Dolphin Pose** → Interlaces fingers or flat palms, builds serratus anterior strength.
*   **Phase 4: Sirsasana A (Supported Headstand)** → Weight distributed 80% on forearms, 20% on crown.
*   **Phase 5: Pincha Mayurasana (Forearm Stand)** → Complete balance on forearms, chest open, ribs knitted.

## 3. Structural Modification Blueprint
When a client hits joint restrictions, map props to alter leverage seamlessly:
*   **Hamstring Shortening:** Place two blocks on high settings under hands in *Ardha Uttanasana* (Half Lift).
*   **Tight Hips/Ankles:** Sit on a folded Mexican blanket or cork block during *Malasana* (Yogi Squat) to keep spine upright.
*   **Sacroiliac (SI) Joint Sensitivity:** Transition from wide-legged stances to parallel setups, avoiding extreme asymmetry in *Warrior II*.
