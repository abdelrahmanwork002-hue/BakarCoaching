# Core Framework: The Structural Mastery Template

Use this blueprint to build personalized daily routines. This structure prioritizes **joint longevity, skill adaptation, and raw neural output** by grouping movements logically.

---

## Phase 1: Joint Preparation & Neural Activation (10-15 Mins)
*Objective: Increase synovial fluid in wrists, elbows, and shoulders while activating the nervous system.*

1. **Wrist Mobility Sequence:** 
   * 15 reps forward/backward rock in quadruped position.
   * 15 reps side-to-side wrist shifts.
2. **Scapular Mechanics:** 
   * 2 sets x 10 Scapular Pull-ups (hang + shrug up).
   * 2 sets x 10 Scapular Push-ups (plank position + collapse and protract space between blades).
3. **Core Compaction:** 
   * 3 sets x 30-second Hollow Body Hold (lower back pressed firmly into floor, hollow chest).

---

## Phase 2: Skill Acquisition & Neurological Focus (15 Mins)
*Objective: Practice high-skill static or dynamic movements while completely fresh. Not performed to failure.*

* **Target Skill Practice:** (e.g., Handstand Kick-ups, Tuck Planche Holds, Frog Stands)
* **Parameters:** 4-6 sets of sub-maximal holds or repetitions. 
* **Rest:** 90-120 seconds between efforts to ensure complete neurological recovery.

---

## Phase 3: Primary Strength Duplets (25 Mins)
*Objective: Build mass, force output, and tendon resilience via paired supersets.*

### Duplet A (Vertical Dynamics)
* **A1. Primary Pulling Variation:** (e.g., Weighted Pull-ups / L-Sit Pull-ups)
  * *Volume:* 4 sets x 5-8 reps
* **A2. Primary Pushing Variation:** (e.g., Wall-Assisted Handstand Push-ups / Deep Dips)
  * *Volume:* 4 sets x 6-8 reps
* *Rest Protocol:* Perform A1, rest 60s, perform A2, rest 90s, repeat.

### Duplet B (Leverage & Horizontal Mastery)
* **B1. Horizontal Pulling:** (e.g., Advanced Tuck Front Lever Rows / Strict Ring Rows)
  * *Volume:* 3 sets x 8-10 reps
* **B2. Horizontal Pushing:** (e.g., Pseudo Planche Push-ups with 2s hold at top)
  * *Volume:* 3 sets x 8-10 reps

---

## Phase 4: Structural Integration & Accessory (10 Mins)
*Objective: Address structural gaps, core compression, and joint balance.*

| Exercise | Target Area | Prescribed Volume |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hanging Leg Raises** | Deep Abs & Hip Flexors | 3 sets x 10-12 reps (no swing) |
| **Reverse Hyperextensions** | Posterior Chain / Lower Back | 3 sets x 15 reps |
| **Banded Face Pulls** | Rear Delts & Rotator Cuff | 3 sets x 20 reps (high volume) |

---

## Phase 5: Decompression & Flexibility (5 Mins)
* **Passive Dead Hang:** 1-2 minutes total time (decompresses spine).
* **German Hang / Skin-the-cat stretch:** 2 sets x 20 seconds (opens anterior shoulder).
