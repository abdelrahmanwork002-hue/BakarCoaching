# Master Macrocycle Plan & Orchestration Framework Template

Use this high-level programmatic blueprint to sync individual training styles into a coherent, phase-based progression cycle where no discipline works against another.

---

## The 12-Week Hybrid Macrocycle Architecture

### Phase 1: Structural Accumulation & Joint Resilience (Weeks 1-4)
* **Primary Objective:** Build connective tissue density in tendons, stabilize core patterns, and establish respiratory control.
* **Volume Matrix:** High Volume / Low-to-Moderate Intensity (Gym RPE 7, Calisthenics basic progressions, Yoga Vinyasa focus).
* **Nutrition Alignment:** Isocaloric or slight surplus to assist structural adaptations.

### Phase 2: Intensification & Kinetic Power Peak (Weeks 5-8)
* **Primary Objective:** Drive maximal absolute force and neurological skill expression.
* **Volume Matrix:** Low Volume / High Intensity (Gym Tier A Power work at RPE 9, Calisthenics advanced leverage holds, Yoga transitions to static Hatha holdings).
* **Nutrition Alignment:** High-protein maintenance; optimal carbohydrate matching around primary strength windows.

### Phase 3: Absolute Integration & Recomposition (Weeks 9-12)
* **Primary Objective:** Transfer structural mass into functional movement efficiency while dialing in body composition.
* **Volume Matrix:** Moderate Volume / High Intent (Complex functional duplets, explosive calisthenics skill integration, deep Yin Yoga and active nervous system decompression).
* **Nutrition Alignment:** Controlled deficit to optimize power-to-weight ratio for bodyweight tracking.

## Cross-Disciplinary Metric Scorecard
The Orchestrator evaluates collective weekly telemetry against these balanced parameters:

| Success Metric | Target Threshold | Warning Flag (Requires Re-Orchestration) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Combined Working Volume** | $\le 16$ working sets per muscle group/week | Exceeds 22 sets/week (Overuse injury pattern) |
| **Autonomic Stress Balance** | Heart Rate Variability (HRV) stable inside baseline | HRV drop $>15\%$ over 3 consecutive days |
| **Nutrition-Recovery Sync** | Caloric intake matches active energy expenditure bounds | Weight drop $>1.5\%$ per week during a strength block |
