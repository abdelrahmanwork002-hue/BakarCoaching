# Core Framework: The Upper / Lower Hypertrophy Split Template

Use this programming matrix to construct balanced, multi-tier gym sessions designed to drive progressive overload while ensuring joint recovery.

---

## Phase 1: Dynamic Warm-Up & CNS Priming (10 Mins)
*Objective: Increase core temperature, lubricate targeted joints, and prime motor unit recruitment.*

1. **Cardiovascular Ramping:** 
   * 5 Minutes on an incline treadmill or rowing machine (RPE 5-6).
2. **Mobility and Activation Circuit:** 
   * 2 sets x 10 reps of Kettlebell Goblet Squats (prying at bottom).
   * 2 sets x 15 reps of Banded Face Pulls (activating rear deltoids and rotator cuff).
3. **Potentiation Lift:** 
   * 1-2 low-rep explosive sets of a medicine ball slam or vertical jump to wake up the nervous system.

---

## Phase 2: Tier A - Structural Compound Power (20 Mins)
*Objective: Focus on maximum mechanical tension using core barbell variations. Complete resting intervals mandatory.*

* **Exercise Selection Example:** Barbell Back Squat OR Conventional Deadlift
* **Prescribed Volume:** 3-4 working sets x 4-6 repetitions.
* **Intensity Target:** RPE 8-9 (2-1 Reps in Reserve).
* **Rest Protocol:** 3 Minutes between sets to ensure full ATP-PC recovery.

---

## Phase 3: Tier B - Hypertrophy Duplets & Accompanying Lifts (25 Mins)
*Objective: Maximize effective volume accumulation and metabolic fatigue using dumbbells or machines.*

### Duplet B (Paired Agonist / Antagonist or Upper Push / Pull)
* **B1. Primary Pressing Variant:** (e.g., Incline Dumbbell Bench Press)
  * *Volume:* 3 sets x 8-10 reps (Controlled 3010 tempo).
* **B2. Primary Pulling Variant:** (e.g., Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row)
  * *Volume:* 3 sets x 10-12 reps (1-second squeeze at peak contraction).

---

## Phase 4: Tier C - Accessory Isolations & Weak Link Fixes (15 Mins)
*Objective: Direct isolated metabolic stress to target tissues while protecting stabilizing joints.*

| Exercise Line | Target Tissue | Prescribed Volume | Intensifier Option |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat** | Quads / Glutes | 3 sets x 10-12 reps per leg | None (Focus on balance) |
| **Standing Cable Lateral Raise** | Lateral Deltoids | 3 sets x 12-15 reps | Drop-set on final set |
| **Hanging Leg Raise** | Rectus Abdominis | 3 sets x Max strict reps | Controlled 3s eccentric |

---

## Phase 5: Localized Down-Regulation & Recovery (5 Mins)
* **Spinal Decompression:** 90-second passive bar hang.
* **Tissue Length Check:** Static stretching of targeted prime movers (e.g., Couch stretch for hip flexors or doorway chest stretch).
