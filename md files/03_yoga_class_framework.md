# Class Blueprint: Vinyasa & Hatha Integrated Flow Template

Use this multi-tier blueprint to build balanced public classes or individual sadhanas. This sequence honors the traditional **Krama (wise progression)** framework to prepare the body safely for peak postures.

---

## Phase 1: Prana Inception & Centering (5-10 Mins)
*Objective: Calm the sympathetic nervous system and establish Ujjayi pranayama.*

1. **Sukasana Grounding:** 
   * Sit tall on a block, hands on knees. 3 minutes of silent observation.
2. **Breath Equalization (Sama Vritti):** 
   * 4-second inhale, 4-second hold, 4-second exhale, 4-second hold. (5 rounds).
3. **Gentle Spinal Wakeup:** 
   * Cat/Cow (Marjaryasana to Bitilasana) focusing on moving segmentally from tailbone to neck.

---

## Phase 2: Surya Namaskar & Systemic Heating (10-15 Mins)
*Objective: Build internal Agni (fire), heat muscle tissues, and sync movement with breath.*

* **Surya Namaskar A (3 Rounds):** 
  * Focus on smooth transitions through *Chaturanga Dandasana* into *Upward Facing Dog*.
* **Surya Namaskar B (2 Rounds):** 
  * Emphasize pelvic stability during the dynamic drop into *Utkatasana* (Chair Pose) and step to *Virabhadrasana I* (Warrior I).

---

## Phase 3: The Asana Krama Flow (25 Mins)
*Objective: Systematically target structural muscle lines leading toward the practice's peak expression.*

### Standing Wave (Hip & Lateral Line Focus)
* **Trikonasana (Triangle Pose):** 
  * Stack shoulders, lengthen bottom side body. Hold for 5 breaths.
* **Utthita Parsvakonasana (Extended Side Angle):** 
  * Deep 90-degree bend in front knee, long line from back heel to fingertips.
* **Vinyasa Reset:** Step back to plank, lower down, open heart, return to Downward Dog.

### Balance & Peak Integration (Neurological Target)
* **Garudasana (Eagle Pose):** 
  * Compresses joints across midline; forces deep internal focus and ankle stabilization.
* **Ardha Chandrasana (Half Moon Pose):** 
  * Transition directly from standing balance to lateral hip opening. Utilize blocks under bottom hand.

---

## Phase 4: Cool-Down Counter-Poses & Integration (10 Mins)
*Objective: Return the nervous system to parasympathetic dominance via static floor poses.*

| Pose | Targeted Muscle Line | Prescribed Hold Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paschimottanasana** | Deep Hamstring / Spinal Extension | 10 Slow Ujjayi Breaths |
| **Eka Pada Rajakapotasana** | Outer Glute / Deep Piriformis Open | 90 Seconds Per Side |
| **Supta Matsyendrasana** | Supine Spinal Detoxifying Twist | 2 Minutes Per Side |

---

## Phase 5: Savasana & Neural Recalibration (7-10 Mins)
* **Passive Release:** Remove all props except a bolster under knees if lower back feels compressed.
* **Somatic Guide:** Guide physical weight into floor, soften jaw, release all breath control, and melt into complete stillness.
