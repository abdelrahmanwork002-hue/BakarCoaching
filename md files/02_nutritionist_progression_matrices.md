# Macronutrient Allocation & Performance Fueling Matrices

Dietary alterations must align dynamically with training stress. Use these calculation frameworks to alter caloric bounds and macronutrient targets across different training load demands.

---

## 1. Dynamic Training-Day Variable Matrix
Scale carbohydrates and absolute calories up or down depending on the day's training pipeline:

```text
[High-Stiffness Strength Day (Gym Tier A / Calisthenics Skills)]
   └── Target: Glycogen Saturation (TDEE + 10% to 15% Calories | High Carb Allocation)

[Active Recovery / Soft-Tissue Day (Yoga Vinyasa Focus)]
   └── Target: Anti-Inflammatory / Muscle Repair (TDEE Calories | Moderate Carb Allocation)

[Complete Localized Deload / Rest Day]
   └── Target: Metabolic System Reset (TDEE - 10% Calories | Low Carb / High Fat Allocation)
```

## 2. Peri-Workout Nutrient Timing Logic
To maximize recovery and muscle building from heavy gym sessions and calisthenics work, implement this structured timing protocol:

### Pre-Workout Window (90-120 Minutes Prior)
* **Goal:** Maximize muscle glycogen, maintain blood glucose, and avoid GI distress.
* **Composition:** Low-fat, low-fiber, moderate protein, complex low-glycemic carbohydrates.
* **Example:** Inverted cream of rice with liquid egg whites OR lean chicken breast with jasmine rice.

### Post-Workout Window (Within 60-90 Minutes Post-Exercise)
* **Goal:** Shift from a catabolic state to an anabolic state, optimizing protein synthesis.
* **Composition:** Fast-acting glycemic carbohydrates combined with rapid-digesting hydrolyzed isolate proteins.
* **Example:** Whey isolate shake paired with a ripe banana or cream of rice.

## 3. Hydration & Intra-Workout Electrolyte Targets
For athletes performing long, high-skill calisthenics or heavy gym sessions:
* **Water Volume:** $1000	ext{mL}$ of water per hour of training.
* **Sodium Target:** $500	ext{mg}$ to $800	ext{mg}$ of elemental sodium dissolved intra-workout to sustain muscular pump and nerve conduction.
* **Potassium/Magnesium Balance:** $200	ext{mg}$ potassium, $100	ext{mg}$ magnesium glycinate.
