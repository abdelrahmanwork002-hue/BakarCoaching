# Multi-Modal Integration Mechanics & Conflict Resolution Matrix

In a hybrid coaching ecosystem, individual domain specialists frequently generate overlapping physical stress. The Senior Orchestrator applies this matrix to resolve structural conflicts before they manifest as overtraining or soft-tissue injury.

---

## 1. Domain Conflict Resolution Tree
When sub-specialists submit plans simultaneously, resolve scheduling and recovery friction using this strict hierarchy:

```text
       [Sub-Specialist Draft Routines Received by Orchestrator]
                                │
         Is Weekly Recovery Capital Exceeded? (Total Volume > Threshold)
              ├── YES ──> [Reduce Accessory Work in Gym & Calisthenics by 30%]
              └── NO  ──> [Proceed to Core System Check]
                                │
          Check Mechanical Overlap: Are Shoulders/Wrists Loaded Consecutively?
              ├── YES ──> [Insert Mandatory 48-Hour Yoga Active Recovery Window]
              └── NO  ──> [Approve & Merge into Macrocycle Layer]
```

---

## 2. Structural Conflict Resolution Rule Matrix

| Detected Micro-Conflict | Trigger Condition | Systemic Resolution Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Heavy Barbell Axial Loading vs. Advanced Inversions** | Gym schedules heavy Back Squats / Deadlifts within 24 hours of Calisthenics or Yoga Handstands. | **Override:** Enforce a minimum 24-hour neural buffer between absolute strength testing and inverted balancing to avoid spinal compression fatigue. |
| **Wrist Extension Structural Exhaustion** | Calisthenics programs Planche holds or Dips while Gym programs heavy Bench and Yoga schedules Arm Balances. | **Override:** Strip all bodyweight arm balance holds from Yoga flow; replace with forearm-grounded modifications (e.g., Dolphin Pose). |
| **Conflicting Metabolic Demands** | Gym sets high-volume hypertrophy sets while Nutrition sets an aggressive 25% caloric deficit. | **Override:** Force Gym rep targets down to lower volume, high-intensity brackets (4-6 reps) to maintain strength while minimizing lean mass wasting. |

## 3. The Cross-Provider Data Validation Pipeline
To ensure automated multi-agent structures do not hallucinate physiologically hazardous prescriptions:
*   **The Content Generation Layer (Creator):** Designs the raw micro-progressions based on pure performance targets.
*   **The Safety Audit Layer (Checker):** Cross-references the combined draft plan against the *Somatic Cross-Check Grid*.
*   **The Multi-Provider Discrepancy Rule:** If the Creator and Checker disagree on loading parameters by more than 15%, the system immediately triggers a state interrupt, locking the plan until the human Senior Coach manually signs off.
