# Master Framework: Weekly Meal Structure & Synchronization Blueprint

Use this structural meal architecture template to organize a client's daily eating timeline. It balances metabolic health with clean performance fueling.

---

## 1. Daily Four-Meal Tier Structure Template

### Meal 1: Metabolic Inception & Tissue Repair (08:00)
* **Objective:** Deliver rapid amino acids to tissue lines after an overnight fast and keep blood sugar stable.
* **Protein Target:** 35-45g lean protein source.
* **Carbohydrate Target:** Fibrous greens and complex low-glycemic starches (e.g., steel-cut oats).
* **Healthy Fat Target:** Essential fatty acids (e.g., whole eggs, avocado, or omega-3 supplementation).

### Meal 2: Pre-Workout Nutrient Loading (13:00)
* **Objective:** Saturate intra-muscular glycogen reserves before loading under the barbell or the pull-up bar.
* **Protein Target:** 40g lean chicken breast, white fish, or ultra-filtered isolates.
* **Carbohydrate Target:** Clean, easily digestible starches (e.g., jasmine rice or sweet potato).
* **Healthy Fat Target:** Kept trace ($\le 5	ext{g}$) to ensure rapid digestion rates.

### Meal 3: Post-Workout Glycogen Replenishment (17:30)
* **Objective:** Replenish lost muscle glycogen stores and trigger rapid protein synthesis.
* **Protein Target:** 40-50g clean protein source.
* **Carbohydrate Target:** Fast-acting, high-glycemic starches to spike insulin (e.g., cream of rice or white potatoes).
* **Healthy Fat Target:** Kept low to avoid delaying nutrient absorption windows.

### Meal 4: Nocturnal Recovery & Anti-Catabolic Reset (21:30)
* **Objective:** Enforce slow amino acid delivery over the sleeping fast to rebuild muscle tissue.
* **Protein Target:** Slow-digesting protein complex (e.g., Micellar Casein or lean beef/cottage cheese).
* **Healthy Fat Target:** Healthy lipids to slow down digestion rates (e.g., extra virgin olive oil, nuts, or almond butter).
